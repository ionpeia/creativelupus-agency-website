Services

Every stage, one crew

We work across the full pipeline, or step into a single stage if that's what a project needs. The order below is roughly the order we work in.

  • 01

    Development

    We work with writer-directors from an early draft through to a financeable package: coverage and structural notes, comparable-title research, and casting attachments.

    • Script coverage and rewrite notes
    • Budget top-sheets and financing plans
    • Casting strategy and attachments
  • 02

    Production

    Line producing and physical production for shorts and low-to-mid budget features, built around crews sized to the actual schedule rather than a standard template.

    • Line producing and scheduling
    • Location, crew, and equipment sourcing
    • On-set supervision and daily reporting
  • 03

    Post-production

    Editorial, sound, and colour handled by a consistent small team, so the film keeps one point of view from assembly through to the final mix.

    • Offline and online editorial
    • Sound design and mix
    • Colour grading and finishing
  • 04

    Delivery & distribution

    Festival strategy, deliverables, and sales support once a film is locked, matched to the size and audience of the project.

    • Festival and sales strategy
    • Technical and marketing deliverables
    • Distributor and platform liaison
